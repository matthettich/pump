Headphones can't be measured with a measurement mic in the open: they need an ear simulator. Load a published measurement of your model (a frequency / dB list, ideally from the same kind of ear simulator the targets assume) or a parametric or graphic EQ file made for it. A file with a target column uses that target with FILE. EQ files are applied as they are, with your tilt, bass and treble on top.
